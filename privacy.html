1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Space Empire ("the App"), developed by Ruslan Podurets ("we", "us", or "the Developer"), collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the App.

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This Policy describes the information processed through the App and the services used to provide its functionality.

2. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the App, the following categories of information may be processed.

2.1 Account Information

If you create or use an account, we process information necessary to identify and manage your account, including:

  • Firebase Authentication user identifier (UID).
  • Account status and account creation or update timestamps.
  • Authentication-related information handled by Firebase Authentication.

We do not use your Firebase UID as your Google Play purchase account identifier. A separate obfuscated account identifier is used for relevant purchase-related operations.

2.2 Game and Virtual Currency Data

To provide game functionality and maintain account progression, we process information such as:

  • Game-related account data.
  • Virtual currency balances, including Galaxy Coins (GC).
  • Purchase-related entitlements and their status.
  • Transaction records, including virtual currency credits and spending.
  • Records of rewarded advertisements and associated rewards.

This information is stored and processed using Google Firebase services.

2.3 Purchase Information

If you make purchases through the App, Google Play processes your payment information. We do not receive or store your full payment card details.

Our backend may process purchase-related information, including:

  • Google Play product identifiers.
  • Purchase tokens required for server-side purchase verification.
  • Purchase status and transaction-related information.
  • Entitlement information associated with completed purchases.

Purchase tokens are protected using encryption in our backend infrastructure. Payment processing is governed by Google's applicable terms and privacy policies.

2.4 Advertising Information

Space Empire may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

Google AdMob may automatically process information necessary to provide, personalize, measure, and protect advertising services. Depending on your consent and applicable settings, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers.
  • Device and technical information.
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it.
  • Advertising interaction and delivery information.
  • Information required for fraud prevention and invalid traffic detection.

We do not directly collect your payment information through advertising services.

2.5 Rewarded Advertisement Verification

When you watch a rewarded advertisement while signed in, the App may send an account identifier to Google AdMob as part of the server-side verification process.

Google AdMob sends a signed verification request to our backend. Our backend validates the request before granting the corresponding virtual currency reward. This process helps prevent fraudulent or duplicate reward claims.

Guests may watch rewarded advertisements without receiving an account-based virtual currency credit.

2.6 Technical and Diagnostic Information

Our infrastructure and third-party service providers may process technical information necessary to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App. This may include server logs, request metadata, error information, and service-related identifiers.

We do not use Firebase Analytics or Google Analytics in Space Empire.

3. How We Use Information

We process information for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing user accounts.
  • Providing game functionality and synchronizing account progression.
  • Maintaining virtual currency balances and transaction history.
  • Processing and verifying purchases.
  • Managing purchased entitlements.
  • Displaying advertisements.
  • Verifying rewarded advertisement completions and granting virtual currency.
  • Preventing fraud, abuse, and unauthorized transactions.
  • Maintaining the security and reliability of the App.
  • Responding to support requests.
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations.

We do not sell your personal information.

6. Data Storage and Security

We use Google Firebase and related Google Cloud infrastructure to store and process information necessary for the operation of Space Empire.

We apply reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. These measures include:

  • Server-side validation of sensitive operations.
  • Access restrictions for backend data.
  • Server-controlled virtual currency and entitlement updates.
  • Encryption of sensitive purchase-related tokens.
  • Verification of rewarded advertisement callbacks.
  • Protection against duplicate transaction and reward processing.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the App and its functionality, maintain account progression and purchased entitlements, keep transaction and security records, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet applicable legal and accounting requirements.

When information is no longer required, it may be deleted or anonymized in accordance with applicable requirements and our retention practices. Some transaction or security records may need to be retained for a limited period even after account deletion.

8. Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your Space Empire account and associated personal data.

To request account deletion, contact ruslan.podurets.apps@gmail.com. Please include sufficient information to identify your account. Do not send passwords or payment card details.

After verifying your request, we will process account deletion in accordance with applicable requirements. Certain information may be retained where required or permitted by law, including records necessary to prevent fraud, resolve disputes, or comply with legal obligations.

Deleting your Space Empire account does not automatically cancel subscriptions or other payment arrangements managed by Google Play. You must manage those separately through Google Play.
